THROW PROJECTION MAPPER ✕

Warp images & video onto any real-world surface, then project it. Here's the 30-second version:

1 · Add a surface — click ＋ Surface (or press A). A green mesh appears on the stage.
2 · Drop in media — drag an image or video straight onto it, or double-click it. Most .mp4 / .webm / .mov play directly; THROW will tell you precisely if a clip needs converting (usually HEVC/AV1 → H.264).
3 · Shape & place it — drag the glowing corners (yellow) to match your surface, or drag anywhere on the surface body to move the whole thing without bending the mesh. Need a curve? Bump Mesh Points to 4×4+ and drag the inner cyan dots.
4 · Or trace it — click ⬡ Trace (or press P) and click around a ceiling panel / beam / window; THROW fits the surface to that outline automatically.
5 · Project — click ⧉ Display to open the projector tab, drag it onto the projector, fullscreen it FIRST, then line everything up from here — every edit syncs live and the picture never shifts after you've aligned it. Esc in the display exits fullscreen.
SUGGESTIONS
• Set Stage to your projector's resolution (e.g. 1920×1080) for crisp output.
• An empty surface shows a calibration target — line it up to the physical surface first, then add media.
• Use Screen or Add blend so black areas project as transparent (light shows, dark vanishes).
• Tangled a warp? R flattens the mesh, C re-centers the surface.
⛶ Outlines draws each surface's footprint on the projector so you can line up to real edges before adding media — toggle with O.
• You opened this file directly — that's fine for editing, but the ⧉ Display tab needs the tiny local server: double-click start.bat and use the tab it opens instead.
